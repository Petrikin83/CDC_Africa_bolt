About CDC Africa

CDC Africa is the regional hub of an international engineering company specialising in advanced ropeway infrastructure. We adapt proven global technology to African realities — fast-growing cities, complex terrain, and infrastructure gaps. Our mission: deliver real mobility systems across Africa, not just concepts.

Africa's growth requires new infrastructure models. Fast-growing cities, complex terrain and limited connectivity demand mobility systems that can be deployed faster, use less land and adapt to real regional conditions.

On-Demand Capacity

Unlike conventional fixed-speed ropeways that idle between peaks, AirBridge® activates capacity precisely on demand — eliminating wasted infrastructure hours and reducing operational cost per journey.

No Roads Required

Towers are the only ground contact. No road clearing, no habitat destruction, no heavy civil works. Installation itself is performed from the sky — no ground access required.

Terrain Access Technology

OutdoorLift for Short-Haul Terrain Access

CDC offers OutdoorLift systems as a complementary access technology for resorts, viewpoints, hillside properties and public destinations where traditional infrastructure is not feasible or would be too intrusive.

The self-propelled outdoor cabin follows the terrain itself — adapting to curves, inclines and natural obstacles — improving accessibility while minimising ground impact.

Low-Impact Access

Flexible routing, lightweight structures and fast non-invasive installation help reduce terrain disruption.

Flexible by Design

Self-propelled cabins adapt to direction and incline changes, with onboard systems and modular configurations.

Safety by Design

Redundant braking, backup power, real-time monitoring and emergency communication support reliable operation.
